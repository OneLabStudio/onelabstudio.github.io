OLPlayer: Music & Video Player

Music and video in one app. Play the formats you use, tune the sound with the equalizer, and make it yours with glass-style themes.

Free to use with ads. OLPlayer Pro is an optional in-app purchase that removes ads and adds premium themes and extra features.

View on Google Play
  • Plays MP3, FLAC, AAC, MP4, MKV and more
  • Equalizer with preamp, presets and your own saved presets
  • Background playback and sleep timer
  • Download subtitles inside the app
  • Popup player and swipe controls for video
  • PIN protection and Safe Mode for settings, deleting and playlists
  • Light and dark themes with custom backgrounds

QR & Barcode Scanner Pro

Scan QR codes and barcodes quickly with a smooth, easy-to-use scanner built for everyday use. Your scan history stays organized on your device, and no account is needed.

Works well for business, online selling, shopping, event check-in and digital access. Requires Android 6.0 or later.

View on Google Play
  • Fast, accurate scanning with your camera
  • Create QR codes and barcodes
  • History of scanned and created codes
  • Mark important codes as favorites
  • Copy results, open links and share codes
  • Camera permission only, data stays on your device

Supports QR Code, Code 128, Code 39, Code 93, Codabar, EAN-13, EAN-8, ITF, UPC-A, UPC-E, PDF417, Aztec and Data Matrix.
