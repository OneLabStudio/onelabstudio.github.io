This Privacy Policy explains how OneLab Studio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information obtained from users (each referred to as a "User") of our Android applications (the "Apps" or "Services").

Information We Collect

We generally do not require personally identifiable information unless clearly stated and provided with your consent. If you contact us directly, we may receive information such as your email address and any information you choose to provide.

Information Collected Automatically

Log Data and Crash Reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information, including device model, operating system version, application version, crash logs, and performance information to help us identify and fix issues.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how users interact with our Apps. This may include information such as sessions, screens viewed, features used, device information, country-level location, app performance data, and engagement metrics.

If advertising is enabled, we may use your device's advertising identifier (such as Google Advertising ID) to support advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising performance analysis.

Our Apps and third-party services used by our Apps, including Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Services, may process IP addresses and network-related information for security, fraud prevention, analytics, advertising measurement, service operation, and advertising personalization where consent has been provided.
