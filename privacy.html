Information We Collect

Our Apps are built with a strong focus on protecting user privacy. We only collect information necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the Services. This helps ensure the Apps function properly and provide a better user experience.

In most cases, you are not required to submit personally identifiable information to use the main features of our Apps. If certain features require such information, it will be clearly explained at the time of collection and only collected with your permission.

Information Collected Automatically

Log Data and Crash Reports

If an error or crash occurs while using our Apps, diagnostic data may be collected through third-party services such as Firebase Crashlytics. This data may include device information, operating system version, app configuration, date and time of the crash, and technical error logs. This information helps us identify issues and improve app stability.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to better understand how users interact with our Apps. This may include information such as how often the app is opened, session duration, features used, device model, operating system version, and general location (country-level). This data is aggregated and used to improve user experience.

Advertising Identifier

If personalized advertising is enabled, the Apps may access your device’s advertising identifier (such as the Google Advertising ID) to deliver more relevant advertisements. You may reset or disable this identifier at any time through your device settings.
