Information We Collect

Our Apps are built with a strong focus on protecting user privacy. We only collect information that is necessary to operate, maintain, and enhance the Service. This information helps us ensure the Apps run smoothly, remain stable, and continue to improve the overall user experience.

If certain functionalities request such information, it will be clearly explained at the time it is collected, and we will only proceed with your explicit permission.

Information Collected Automatically (Usage Data and Device Information)

Log Data and Crash Reports

If an error or crash occurs while using our Apps, we may collect diagnostic information through third-party services such as Firebase Crashlytics. This information can include details about the device, operating system version, app settings, the date and time the issue occurred, and technical error logs. The purpose of collecting this data is to help us identify problems and improve the stability and performance of the Apps.

Analytics Data

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to better understand how users interact with our Apps. This may include information such as how often the app is opened, which features are used, session duration, device model, operating system version, and general location information (for example, country level). The data is collected in aggregated form and helps us enhance the overall user experience.

Advertising Identifier

If you allow personalized advertising, our Apps may access your device’s advertising identifier (such as the Google Advertising ID) to provide more relevant advertisements. You can reset or restrict the use of this identifier at any time through your device’s privacy or advertising settings.
